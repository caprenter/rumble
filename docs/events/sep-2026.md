**Please Note: This is event is in the Main Theatre on the ground floor at the Arts Centre**

We've got the 'Queen Rig' and capacity for 500 people for one night only (or hopefully for the first of many events), so come down to see the other side of the Arts Centre and what might be the future of grassroots music in our town.

The Queen Rig is set up for a tribute act who are playing the following night - and includes a set of stage speakers that were used at Live Aid back in the day!
